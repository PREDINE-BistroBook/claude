# 100 enhancement ideas

Brainstormed from six angles (agency, school, AI automation, marketing, owner/finance/safety, differentiators), then deduplicated against what is already built or planned. The school marks its picks on the interactive Idea Board: https://claude.ai/artifact/SV4PHBRaWH7aG4wBYD4Q1Y

★ = top pick (best payoff for a zero budget). Cost: Free, or AI pennies (Claude Haiku).

## Admissions & school operations

**1. ★ One-click next-term renewals** — At term end, the app suggests each student's next step (move up, repeat, or join the agency track) based on their coach scores and attendance. It writes a personal renewal message with an early-bird deadline, ready to send through a wa.me WhatsApp link. A live list shows who has renewed, who hasn't, and who needs a call from Salma. *Payoff:* Renewals are the cheapest money the school earns, and they no longer depend on Salma remembering to chase every family. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**2. Free trial class booking page** — Each week, one or two seats in real classes are kept as trial seats. A public mini-page on Cloudflare's free tier lists these slots, with seat counts taken live from the timetable. A parent picks a slot (even at midnight), goes straight onto Salma's leads list, and gets a WhatsApp confirmation and reminder link. After class, the coach rates the trial student on three quick sliders, and staff mark who turned up. *Payoff:* Unsure families can try a class before paying, Salma stops trading messages to arrange trials, and the owner sees how many trials become paying students. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**3. Smart waitlist with automatic offers** — Full classes keep a ranked waitlist. When someone drops out, the app picks the next person, prepares the WhatsApp offer and gives them 48 hours to accept before moving down the list. When many people are waiting for the same time slot, it suggests opening a new group. *Payoff:* Empty seats are filled again within days instead of sitting unpaid, and the owner knows a new group will fill before he opens it. _(Small effort · Free)_

**4. Best class and coach suggestion** — After Salma's interview, the app suggests the two best class-and-coach options for the new student. It weighs age, level, goals, dialect, preferred times, open seats, each coach's current workload, and how similar past students progressed with each coach. Salma sees the reasons and makes the final choice with one tap. *Payoff:* Placing a student takes minutes instead of juggling spreadsheets, coach workloads stay fair, and better first matches mean fewer students quit early. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**5. Success stories screen for interviews** — During an admissions interview, Salma opens a screen that shows success stories matched to the family in front of her: same age group, same goal (TV ads, series, theatre or confidence) and same part of Cairo. It pulls them from the bookings, progress reels and testimonials already in the app. *Payoff:* Salma signs up more families because she can show proof that fits them, using material the school already has. _(Small effort · Free)_

**6. Interview co-pilot for Salma** — Before an interview, the AI turns the applicant's form into six questions suited to that person (a shy 12-year-old needs different questions from a 25-year-old changing careers). Afterwards, Salma speaks or types rough notes, and the AI turns them into a tidy assessment on the school's criteria, ready for placement. *Payoff:* Interviews are faster and follow the same standard whoever runs them, and no assessment is left half-written. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**7. Open day and intake audition mode** — For open days and big intake days, the app creates a Google Form for sign-ups, a printable QR poster for the door and a check-in screen on a staff phone. Applicants get a queue number and are split across rooms, and judges score each person on their phones in about 30 seconds. By evening the app has ranked everyone and prepared acceptance messages. The next morning, staff get a list of personal WhatsApp follow-ups for every visitor. *Payoff:* Handles 50 or more visitors without paper sign-in sheets that never get typed up, and every visitor hears from the school within 24 hours, while they are still keen. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**8. Makeup class finder** — When a student misses a session, the app lists other groups at the same level with free seats this week and prepares a WhatsApp message offering the slot. Each student has a balance of makeup credits so the option isn't abused. *Payoff:* Fewer students fall behind or feel they wasted sessions they paid for, which is one of the main reasons parents ask for refunds or quit. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**9. One-tap substitute coach** — A coach marks themselves unavailable, and the app shows which qualified coaches are free at that time and sends them a WhatsApp request link. The coach who accepts automatically gets the class list and a note of where the class left off. *Payoff:* No more cancelled classes or frantic phone calls an hour before class, which protects both income and the school's reputation. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**10. Ramadan, Eid and exam-aware timetable** — Mark the year's seasons once: Ramadan, the Eid breaks, and Thanaweya Amma and school exam periods. During Ramadan, classes and auditions move to after iftar automatically, using free prayer-time calculations for Cairo. Reminders are never sent during iftar or Friday prayer, terms are extended fairly to cover holidays, and notices to families are prepared automatically. *Payoff:* Saves days of rescheduling by hand every year, cuts no-shows, and avoids arguments about paid sessions 'lost' to holidays. _(Medium effort · Free)_

## Coaching & student growth

**11. ★ Voice notes become coach evaluations** — After class, the coach taps a microphone button and talks for 20 to 60 seconds about each student, in Egyptian Arabic or English. The phone's free built-in dictation turns the speech into text, and the AI sorts it into the school's evaluation criteria with draft scores and one clear note. The coach adjusts any score and saves with one tap. *Payoff:* An evaluation takes about 2 minutes instead of 15 minutes of typing, so evaluation records fill up every week. Parent letters, promotions and actor rankings then all work from better information. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**12. Every audition teaches something** — If there is no news five days after an audition, the app drafts a polite message asking the casting director for feedback, and the booker approves it before it goes. Staff tap quick reasons (too young, weak dialect, low energy, poor self-tape) or record a voice note. The AI turns these into notes on the student's profile and a focus task on their coach's list. Each month it shows which skill gaps cost the most bookings and suggests workshop topics. *Payoff:* Even a 'no' improves the training, so coaches work on exactly what casting directors notice, and more auditions turn into bookings. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**13. Kombars-to-Batal level ladder** — The school's levels are named after real industry roles: Kombars (extra), Small Role, Supporting and Batal (lead). Each level lists the skills and minimum scores it needs. Every student's progress bar fills from coach scores, attendance and homework, and at term end the app flags who has earned a promotion. Coaches pick a monthly star student, and each level-up creates a ready-to-post Instagram story image. *Payoff:* Students and parents see a clear path worth paying for term after term, promotions are no longer argued case by case, and every level-up students share is free advertising. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**14. Homework self-tapes through Telegram** — Coaches set a monologue or scene with a deadline. Students send their self-tape to the school's free Telegram bot or through a Google Form, it lands on the coach's review list automatically, and the coach's short comment goes back to the student. Missed homework is fed into the dropout warning list. *Payoff:* Students practise between classes and improve faster, and coaches review everything in one place instead of searching through WhatsApp chats. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**15. AI scene partner on the phone** — A student pastes or photographs a scene or their audition pages and picks their character. The AI splits the lines by character. The phone's free built-in voice reads the other parts aloud in Arabic or English, waits while the student speaks, and counts lines that were dropped or changed. At the end, a short report shows the trouble spots. *Payoff:* Students can rehearse or record a self-tape at midnight without a classmate and arrive word-perfect, so class time goes on acting instead of memorising. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**16. Monologue matchmaker and shared library** — A shared library of monologues and scenes in Egyptian colloquial Arabic, classical Arabic and English, tagged by age, gender, length, genre, level and whether it suits children. For each student, the AI picks three pieces that fit their type and work on their weakest evaluation scores, explains why, and remembers who has already performed which piece. *Payoff:* Coaches stop spending evenings hunting for material, children never get unsuitable scripts, and showcases avoid three students doing the same piece. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**17. Dialect and Fusha practice gym** — A coach pastes a scene in Egyptian Arabic, and the AI rewrites it in Saudi, Levantine, Sa'idi or Fusha with a list of the words that changed. For Fusha, it adds full vowel marks (tashkeel) and highlights the letters Egyptians tend to soften (qaf, tha, dhal). Students read the drills aloud, and the phone's free speech recognition shows in red any word it couldn't understand. Coaches can see each student's practice streak. *Payoff:* Students get ready for Gulf productions, historical dramas and dubbing tests, and coaches save hours marking up scripts by hand. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**18. Exam day jury mode** — For assessment days, the app builds the time slots (for example 8 minutes per student) and puts each jury member's score sheet on their phone, using the level's criteria. It averages the scores as they come in and produces pass, promote and repeat lists the same evening. *Payoff:* Exam days run on time with no paper score sheets, and results are fair and easy to explain if a parent questions them. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**19. Session log and lesson library** — After each class, the coach spends under a minute ticking what was covered (warm-ups, exercises, scenes) from a shared list. Over time this builds the school's own course plan, showing which exercises each level does and in what order. *Payoff:* The school owns its teaching method, substitute coaches can pick up exactly where a class stopped, and quality stays the same whoever is teaching. _(Medium effort · Free)_

## Students, parents & community

**20. ★ Monthly parent progress letter** — Each month, the AI writes a short, warm note to each student's parents in Egyptian Arabic (or English on request). It draws on attendance, coach scores, voice-note feedback, homework and any auditions, and names one strength, one thing to practise at home and what's coming next. Salma skims it and sends it with one tap through a wa.me WhatsApp link. *Payoff:* Parents see what they are paying for, which leads to renewals and recommendations, and it takes Salma minutes instead of an evening. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**21. ★ Dropout early warning with check-in draft** — Every week, the app flags students who seem to be drifting away: two missed classes in a row, skipped homework, a late payment, falling scores or worried wording in coach notes. For each one, Salma gets a one-line reason and a caring check-in message or call script, and decides whether to call, send it or leave it. *Payoff:* Keeping even two students a month from quietly quitting saves thousands of pounds of tuition that would otherwise disappear without anyone noticing. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**22. Referral codes and bring-a-friend days** — Each student and parent gets a personal code and a one-tap WhatsApp share button with a ready-written Arabic message. Once a month there is a 'bring a friend to class' session, with guests registered at the door. When a new enquiry uses a code or comes as a guest, the person who referred them gets credit and a reward the owner sets (for example a free masterclass seat). A leaderboard shows the top referrers. *Payoff:* Word of mouth is how Egyptian families choose activities, and this makes it measurable and rewarded instead of left to chance. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**23. One-tap guardian consent per job** — For every audition or booking involving a child, the app creates a WhatsApp link to a simple page showing the role, dates, hours, location, pay and any sensitive content (type of scene, smoking, physical contact, night shooting). The parent types their name and taps Approve or Decline. The answer is saved with the date and time, and the child can't move forward without it. *Payoff:* Ends chasing parents by phone, gives written proof that parents agreed to every job, and builds the trust that brings more young students to the school. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**24. End-of-term showcase planner** — Plan each showcase in one place. The AI suggests a running order that gives every student fair stage time and shows off their casting type. The app spots actors cast in two scenes at once, adds rehearsals to the weekly schedule, tracks props and costumes, and makes a bilingual poster. Each student gets a personal invite link for their family, with sign-up counts and seat limits. *Payoff:* The most stressful week of the term becomes a checklist, and a full, polished showcase is the school's best advert to the parents and friends watching. _(Large effort · AI pennies)_

**25. Monthly class highlight for parents** — Each month, the editors cut a 60-second highlight for each class, using only footage of students who have consent. It goes to that class's parents as a private link in a one-tap WhatsApp message, with the school watermark and a trial-class link for friends. *Payoff:* Parents feel connected to what they pay for, and when they proudly share it on their own stories, it reaches new families for free. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**26. Shareable bilingual certificates** — At term end, the app prints Arabic and English certificates in bulk showing the level, hours attended and coach. Each has a QR code that opens a page confirming it is genuine. It also makes an Instagram story-size version with the student's photo (if consent allows) and the school's account name, for parents to download and share. *Payoff:* Proud families post them at the same moment, which spreads the school's name for free, and fake certificates can't trade on the school's name. _(Small effort · Free)_

**27. Alumni mentor matching** — Former students who now work as actors offer one 30-minute call a month and list their genres and dialects. The app matches current students to the right mentor and logs the sessions. Mentors earn free workshop places and a 'Mentor' badge on their profile card. *Payoff:* Gives the school a premium extra worth charging more for, at no cost, and keeps working alumni close for referrals and casting tips. _(Small effort · Free)_

## Casting & talent discovery

**28. ★ Script-to-roles breakdown in one click** — When a production sends a full script or story outline (a PDF or pasted text), the AI lists every character with age range, gender, dialect, look, number of scenes and a one-line description. It then fills in a new casting with those roles. The booker removes what isn't needed, and the app's existing actor matching runs on each role. *Payoff:* A 2-hour reading job becomes 5 minutes, and the agency can put actors forward for small and supporting roles it would otherwise miss. More submissions per production means more bookings. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**29. Ready-for-roster gate and handoff** — A checklist shows what each student still needs before joining the agency's roster: the right level, minimum coach scores, attendance and punctuality, headshots, a showreel, an approved self-tape, consent forms and a complete profile. Each month, the AI suggests students who look ready, with a short reason. When the owner and his father approve, the app opens headshot and showreel jobs for the editors and starts a draft talent profile. *Payoff:* Ready students start getting bookings (and earning commission for the agency) months sooner, only polished actors reach casting directors, and students have a clear goal that keeps them enrolled. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**30. Casting director pick-list link** — Instead of sending actors one at a time, the agent sends one link per role showing the 5 to 10 best actors. Without logging in, the casting director taps Yes, Maybe, No or Send self-tape on each actor, and the answers move actors along the submissions pipeline automatically. If the link isn't opened, the agent gets a reminder. For WhatsApp, the app can also make one clean image of all the headshots, with names, age ranges and key skills in the agency's colours. *Payoff:* Casting directors get a faster, more professional service in the format they already use, and the agency learns exactly who they liked instead of hearing nothing back. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**31. Showcase night that books actors** — Invite casting directors to showcases straight from the relationship book, with sign-up links and QR tickets. The printed or on-phone programme has a QR code for each performer that opens their profile card. Casting directors tap 'Interested', and each tap becomes a follow-up task and moves that student into the castings pipeline. *Payoff:* Every school performance turns into real casting leads, which links the school and the agency into one money-making loop. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**32. Dialect passport and verified skills** — Each actor gets a grid of dialects (Cairene, Alexandrian, Sa'idi, Bedouin, Saudi/Gulf, Levantine, Fusha) and special skills (English accent, horse riding, swimming, driving, dance, martial arts, musical instruments). Each entry has a level, a 15 to 20 second proof clip and the name of the coach who checked it. Levels go out of date after 6 months, which prompts a quick re-check in class, and role searches can be limited to verified skills only. *Payoff:* When a casting asks for 'a convincing Sa'idi father' or 'a Gulf-dialect speaker who rides horses', the agency can send proven matches within minutes and avoid embarrassing surprises on set. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**33. Family and look-alike group finder** — Egyptian ads often need a believable family (father, mother, two children) or siblings who look alike. Describe the group, and the AI suggests sets of actors from the roster who match in age, look and skin tone, ready to send as one package. *Payoff:* Answers a common, tricky ad request in minutes that rival agencies struggle with, which wins well-paid commercial bookings. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**34. Voice-over and dubbing talent line** — A voice library where each actor records short samples (narration, a cartoon character, an ad read, Gulf dialect), tagged by tone and age. Staff can send a dubbing studio or ad agency a link to five matching voices in seconds. *Payoff:* Opens a new income stream from dubbing studios, ad agencies and cartoons, using students the school already has. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**35. Host casting days at the school** — Offer the school's studio free to casting directors for their audition days. The app books the room and sends call times. At the door, a tablet lets each actor scan the QR code from their invitation (or type their phone number), take a fresh photo and sign consent on screen. A 'now calling' screen runs the queue, and the casting director sees each actor's card. Recordings go to Google Drive, and every walk-in actor is added to the database. *Payoff:* Casting directors who audition in your building see your actors first and keep coming back, and the actor database grows for free. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**36. Headshot auto-tagger and quality check** — The AI looks at every new headshot and adds searchable tags, such as beard, glasses, hijab, smiling or serious, and looks like 'doctor', 'street' or 'upper-class'. It flags blurry photos, heavy beauty filters, sunglasses and busy backgrounds. Staff accept the tags with one tap, and actors with flagged photos get a 'retake needed' task. *Payoff:* Searches like 'bearded man, rough look' find the right faces straight away, and casting directors stop receiving filtered photos that hurt the agency's credibility. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

## Agency & bookings

**37. ★ Casting director relationship book** — A card for every casting director and production company. It shows every request they sent, who the agency put forward, who they called in and booked, their feedback, what they paid and how quickly, and their tastes ('prefers natural headshots'). Before anyone replies to them, the AI shows a short summary of that history. Each week, the app lists good clients nobody has contacted in 60 days and suggests three actors to pitch to each. *Payoff:* One-off jobs become repeat business, pitches match each client's taste, and relationships don't depend on one person's memory. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**38. Casting rush board with Ramadan mode** — Every incoming casting request shows a countdown from the moment it arrives, aiming for a first shortlist within 2 hours. The board shows who owns each request and which are overdue, plus the average response time each month. In the casting season before Ramadan, the owner switches on Rush Mode. It adds a countdown to Ramadan and a daily 'casting now' board, moves self-tape jobs to the front of the editors' queue, and locks actors already committed to long shoots so nobody pitches them twice. *Payoff:* The first agency to reply often gets the booking, and this wins more work in the few months that bring in most of the year's casting money. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**39. Booking message to call sheet** — When a casting director confirms a booking in a long WhatsApp or email message, staff paste it in. The AI pulls out the date, call time, location, wardrobe, lines, fee, overtime terms and contact person. It drafts a clear Arabic/English confirmation for the production and a message for the actor (and the guardian, for a child), with a Google Maps link and a calendar file. The night before the shoot, the actor gets a Telegram reminder with a 'Confirmed' button, and staff can see who hasn't confirmed. *Payoff:* Actors no longer turn up at the wrong place or time, there are fewer arguments over vague terms and fewer no-shows, and productions keep coming back. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**40. Extras booking desk** — A fast lane for productions that need 20 to 100 extras at short notice. Staff post the call (date, look, pay) to a free Telegram channel, and the first people to confirm fill the list. On set, staff take attendance by phone, and the app works out each person's fee and the agency's cut. *Payoff:* Extras work is regular, high-volume income in Egyptian production, and this turns a chaotic phone scramble into a 30-minute job. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**41. Book-out and holds calendar** — Each actor has a calendar showing days they are away or already booked, plus days a production has pencilled them in (first hold, second hold). When staff put an actor forward for a casting with known shoot dates, the app warns about clashes, and it reminds staff to chase a production whose hold is about to run out. *Payoff:* Stops double-booking and stops the agency putting forward someone who turns out to be busy, which protects its name with casting directors. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**42. Travel-ready board for Gulf jobs** — Each actor's profile records passport expiry, visas already held (Saudi, UAE), whether they are willing to travel for 1 to 3 months, and their Gulf dialect level. One filter answers 'who can fly to Riyadh within 10 days?', and passport expiry alerts go out 6 months ahead. *Payoff:* The agency can say yes to short-notice, well-paid Gulf bookings instead of losing them over an expired passport. _(Small effort · Free)_

**43. Contracts filled from the record** — Arabic/English templates for enrolment terms, talent representation agreements (commission % and length), guardian permissions and freelance coach and editor agreements are filled in automatically from the app's records. They can then be printed or sent as a PDF. A photo of the signed copy is attached to the record, and the owner and his father are reminded before a representation agreement expires. *Payoff:* Every commission is backed in writing, and agreements get renewed before an actor drifts to another agency. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**44. Actor's private status page** — Every actor gets a private, hard-to-guess link that shows their upcoming auditions with 'Confirm' and 'Can't make it' buttons, how far each submission has got, and any missing documents. Their taps update the app and build a reliability score from confirmations, punctuality, missed auditions or self-tape deadlines, and any complaints from productions. Staff see the score before shortlisting. *Payoff:* Cuts the daily flood of 'any news?' WhatsApp messages, and the agency only puts forward actors who are known to turn up. _(Small effort · Free)_

**45. Monthly New Faces lookbook** — Once a month, the app gathers students newly added to the roster and fresh headshots into one branded web page, grouped by age and type. It is emailed to the casting director and production company list through the free Gmail connection, with a reply link to request auditions. *Payoff:* Keeps the agency in casting directors' minds every month without staff writing pitches. _(Medium effort · Free)_

## Video & content production

**46. Showreel gap finder and paid shoots** — The AI compares the scenes in an actor's showreel with the roles they keep being put forward for, and suggests the missing scene (for example 'no comedy' or 'no Upper Egyptian character'). The school sells an in-house shoot of that scene, and the job goes into the editors' queue automatically. *Payoff:* Actors get showreels that win auditions, and the school earns from a steady paid service using equipment and editors it already has. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**47. Day 1 vs today progress reels** — Each student's class clips and self-tapes (Google Drive links) sit on a timeline. Using the coaches' score history, the app finds students who have improved a lot and pairs a first-month clip with a recent one. That becomes a ready editing task for a before-and-after reel, which is always sent privately to the family and posted publicly only with consent. *Payoff:* Seeing their own child's progress persuades parents to renew, and these clips are the most convincing ads an acting school can run. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**48. Guided self-tape recorder** — A camera inside the app with an outline for framing, an automatic name card at the start (name, height, age range, agency logo, role), scrolling text of the other character's lines for the person reading opposite, and a checklist (light, quiet, phone held sideways). The finished take goes straight to Google Drive and the editors' queue. *Payoff:* Editors get far fewer unusable tapes to rescue, so self-tapes go out the same day in busy casting weeks. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**49. Footage library searchable by student** — Coaches upload exercise clips from their phones through the app, straight into Google Drive folders sorted by class and date. They tag the students in each clip with one tap from that day's attendance list, so anyone can pull up 'every clip of Youssef' or 'all improvisation exercises this month'. *Payoff:* Editors stop digging through WhatsApp videos, and the same footage gets used for showreels, progress reels and marketing. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**50. Booked! story drafted automatically** — When a submission reaches 'booked', the app opens a content task. The AI drafts a caption in Egyptian Arabic and English, and the editor gets the student's earliest class clip to build a 'from our classroom to the set' reel. The post is held until the date the production allows it to be shown. *Payoff:* Every booking, the school's strongest proof, becomes a post without anyone having to remember, and nobody breaks a production's secrecy by posting too early. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**51. Digitals day and look-change tracker** — Staff take standard no-makeup photos (front, profile, full length) inside the app, with an on-screen outline so everyone is framed the same way, and record measurements. Every two months, actors upload three selfies. The AI flags changes (a beard, shorter hair, weight change, now wears hijab), updates the look tags, and flags photos older than 6 months for a monthly photo day. *Payoff:* Casting directors get up-to-date, consistent photos without the school paying a photographer, and every flagged profile is a chance to sell a paid headshot session. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**52. Showreel brief writer for editors** — When a showreel or self-tape job is created, the AI writes the editor a clear brief from the actor's profile and the request. It covers target length, which roles and emotions to open with, which clips to use (with Drive links and the coaches' notes on the best moments), the name card text, the music mood and the subtitle language. The person who asked for the job checks the brief before it joins the queue. *Payoff:* Editors stop going back and forth on WhatsApp asking what is wanted, which means fewer rounds of changes on every reel. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**53. Monthly content shoot-day planner** — The app plans one batch filming day a month. It picks students with consent who need showreel or self-tape material, matches them with what the content calendar needs, builds a shot list and a timetable, and checks that the editors have enough free hours that week. *Payoff:* One organised shoot covers paid showreel work and marketing content together, saving editor and coach hours and studio setup time. _(Medium effort · Free)_

## Marketing & social media

**54. ★ Cold lead revival and win-back** — Each enquiry that didn't enrol is given a reason (price, schedule, distance, age, 'thinking about it'). Every day, the app lists who to follow up, with an AI-drafted personal message that refers to what they asked about ('you asked about the kids' class, and a new group starts Saturday at 5'), sent through a wa.me link. Before each term, it matches old enquiries to changes, for example 'you wanted weekends, and we now have a Friday class'. *Payoff:* Wins back families the school already put effort into attracting, which are usually the cheapest new students it will ever get. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**55. Our students on screen** — Each booked role is recorded with the show name, the channel or platform (Shahid, WATCH IT, MBC Masr) and the air date. On the night it airs, the app reminds the team to post a 'spot our student' story and adds the role to a public 'our students on screen' wall. After Ramadan and Eid, it makes ready Arabic/English posters ('23 of our actors in 11 series this Ramadan') that include only actors whose consent allows it. *Payoff:* Every Ramadan season becomes free advertising just when viewers dream of acting, and the wall wins the trust of new families and production companies. _(Small effort · Free)_

**56. Google review booster** — At happy moments (a great evaluation, a first showcase, a booking), the app prompts staff to send a one-tap WhatsApp message asking the family for a Google review, with the review link included, and records who has already been asked. Each week, the AI also drafts a short post for the school's free Google Business profile. *Payoff:* More reviews and fresh posts push the school up 'acting classes near me' searches in Maadi, New Cairo or Sheikh Zayed, which brings in enquiries for free. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**57. Weekly post ideas with safe captions** — Every Monday, the AI reads what happened at the school that week (new students, big jumps in scores, bookings, upcoming showcases, birthdays). It suggests five post ideas, each with an opening line, Arabic/English captions and hashtags. Before any photo is used, the app checks the consent of every student pictured. Staff approve each idea, assign it to an editor and post it themselves. Nothing is posted automatically. *Payoff:* Editors never face a blank page, content comes from the school's real successes, and no child whose parents said no is ever shown. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**58. Testimonial bank and quote cards** — A short Google Form collects written or video testimonials from parents and students into Google Drive. The AI tags each one by theme (a shy child gained confidence, booked a first job, an adult changed career) and turns the best into bilingual quote images in the school's style. *Payoff:* Editors have a ready supply of praise from real families, and Salma can show a worried parent a testimonial from someone just like them. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**59. Egyptian-season content calendar** — A shared calendar with the moments that matter in Egypt already filled in: Ramadan series season, both Eids, the summer holidays, back to school, the El Gouna and Cairo film festivals, Mother's Day on 21 March and the Thanaweya Amma results. Six weeks before each one, it suggests a campaign theme and creates draft content tasks for the editors. *Payoff:* Marketing stops being last-minute, so summer kids' courses are promoted when parents are actually deciding. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**60. Self-updating link-in-bio page** — A free Cloudflare page for the Instagram and TikTok profile link that pulls live from the app. It shows the next trial classes, the upcoming open day or showcase, the latest booked students (where consent allows) and an apply button. Nobody has to edit it by hand. *Payoff:* The profile link is never out of date, and every visitor from social media finds a current reason to sign up. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**61. Follower counts on actor profiles** — Actors update their Instagram and TikTok follower counts once a month through a simple form, because the app can't read them automatically. ## Money & admin

**62. ★ Booking money ledger and fee guide** — When a role is booked, the app opens a money record: the agreed fee, the agency's commission %, when the production should pay, what it has paid so far, and the actor's share and payout date. The AI can read the fee and terms from the confirmation message. An overdue list ('Production X owes 45,000 EGP, 62 days late') comes with polite Arabic/English reminders that an owner approves before they go. Each actor gets a statement they can be sent, and past bookings become a private price guide ('your last 8 lead roles in ads averaged X EGP, and this offer is 30% lower'). *Payoff:* Commission stops being forgotten or paid late, agents negotiate with real numbers, and the owner and his father see exactly what the agency earns compared with tuition. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**63. ★ Payment screenshot reader with copy guard** — Parents already send InstaPay and Vodafone Cash screenshots on WhatsApp. Staff forward the screenshot into the app, and the AI reads the amount, date, sender and transaction number and suggests which student it belongs to. Staff tap confirm, the payment is recorded and a receipt is drafted. If the same transaction number appears a second time, the app blocks it and alerts the owner and his father. *Payoff:* Recording a payment takes seconds instead of minutes, and nobody can claim a new payment by resending an old screenshot. _(Medium effort · AI pennies)_

**64. ★ Attending-but-unpaid student alerts** — The app compares attendance with payments and flags students who keep coming to class while their balance is overdue. Only office staff see the flag, and Salma gets a short daily list with ready-made WhatsApp reminder links. *Payoff:* Recovers tuition that quietly slips away every month, without embarrassing any student in front of their class. _(Small effort · Free)_

**65. ★ Coach and editor payroll in one tap** — At month end, the app counts every session each coach actually taught (from the attendance already taken) and every finished video each editor delivered. It multiplies these by each person's agreed rate, adds substitute cover, bonuses and advances, and produces a payslip for the owner and his father to approve. Payslips can be sent as a WhatsApp link or printed. *Payoff:* Saves the owner a day of counting every month, ends 'I taught 14 sessions, not 12' arguments, and stops the school paying for classes that never happened. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**66. Ad usage-rights and exclusivity watchdog** — For every ad booking, staff record where the ad can be shown (TV, online, Egypt only or the whole Gulf), for how long, and any ban on working for competitors (for example, no other telecom brand for 12 months). The app warns 30 days before the usage period ends so the agency can bill for a renewal, and it flags any attempt to put that actor forward to a competing brand. *Payoff:* Ad renewals are close to free money that agencies often miss, and the clash check stops a breach that could cost the agency a client. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**67. One price list, approved discounts only** — All fees (course levels, registration, showreel packages, headshot sessions, private coaching) are kept in one price list with start dates and a record of past prices. Invoices can only use prices from the list, and any price change needs the owner's approval. Every discount, sibling price or scholarship needs a reason and approval from the owner and his father. A monthly total shows what discounts cost and who granted each one. *Payoff:* Staff can't quote different prices on WhatsApp, 'friendly' discounts stop quietly eating into revenue, and the owner sees what generosity really costs. _(Small effort · Free)_

**68. Numbered receipts that can't vanish** — Every payment produces an Arabic/English receipt with a running serial number, sent to the parent on WhatsApp as an image. A receipt can only be cancelled with a written reason, and it stays visible as 'cancelled' instead of being deleted. *Payoff:* Parents get proof straight away, 'I already paid' arguments end, and a missing number reveals money that was taken but never recorded. _(Small effort · Free)_

**69. End-of-day cash closing** — Every staff member who takes money (cash, InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, bank transfer) closes their day in the app. They compare what the app says they collected with what they actually hand over, and explain any gap. The owners see a running total of the money each person is holding until it is handed in. *Payoff:* The owner and his father always know where every pound is, and small losses or mistakes show up the same day instead of months later. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**70. Expenses, custody and accountant pack** — Staff log expenses (taxis to auditions, props, printing) with a photo of the receipt, and cash advances held by staff (عهدة) are tracked until they are settled. Spending over a limit the owner sets needs approval from the owner and his father, and each category has a monthly budget that warns at 80%. One button exports the month's income, expenses, payroll and commissions into a Google Sheet laid out the way the accountant wants. *Payoff:* Ends untracked petty cash, gives the family a real monthly spending total for the first time, and saves days of gathering papers for the accountant. _(Medium effort · Free)_

## AI assistants & automation

**79. ★ Reply drafts from the fact sheet** — The owner keeps one editable fact sheet with prices, class ages, schedules, the trial class, the location and what to bring. It writes in the style the person used (Egyptian Arabic, Arabic typed in English letters and numbers, or English) and says 'not sure' rather than making something up. Staff edit the draft and copy it into WhatsApp, and approved replies are saved as examples so later drafts get better. *Payoff:* The 20 questions asked every day are answered in seconds, and a tired staff member never quotes the wrong price. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**80. Screenshot a DM, get a lead** — Instagram and Facebook messages can't be read automatically, so staff take a screenshot of a private message and drop it into the app. The AI creates an admissions enquiry (name, phone, child or adult, age, interest, source: Instagram message) and suggests a reply in Egyptian Arabic with the next real trial date and prices already filled in. *Payoff:* Social media enquiries stop getting lost in someone's phone, and each one becomes a tracked enquiry in seconds. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**81. One-tap 'Are you free?' check** — Before putting actors forward, the agent sends the role summary and dates to the shortlisted actors through a free Telegram bot. Each actor answers with one button: 'I'm in', 'Not available' or 'Call me'. **82. **83. Plain-Arabic contract explainer** — When a production sends a contract or release form, the AI summarises it in plain Arabic for the actor or parent. **84. ## Safety, privacy & trust

**85. Casting scam and child-safety screen** — The AI checks every incoming casting call or booking request for warning signs common in Egypt: asking actors to pay a 'registration' or 'photo' fee, no named production company, auditions in a private flat, late-night shoots for children, or requests for swimwear photos. Each request gets a red, amber or green badge with the reasons, and red ones need an owner's sign-off before any actor is contacted. *Payoff:* Protects students, especially children, and the school's reputation from fake castings, and gives parents a solid reason to trust this agency. _(Small effort · AI pennies)_

**86. Chaperone gate for child jobs** — A child can't be moved to audition or booked until a named guardian or approved chaperone is assigned and has confirmed. On the day, the chaperone checks the child in and out on their phone and can see the child's emergency contacts and medical notes, even without internet. Jobs that break the school's own limits (late nights, school days, long hours) are flagged. *Payoff:* Protects children and the business from a serious incident, and gives parents a clear reason to trust the agency with their children. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**87. Children's photo and clip protection** — Every photo or clip is tagged with the students who appear in it, and the app checks each one's filming and social media consent before an editor can mark it 'OK to post'. If consent is 'parents only', 'never' or out of date, the clip is blocked or the face is flagged for blurring. Children's photos are hidden from staff who don't need them and carry a watermark when shared. Profile card links for children stop working after a set number of days, and every view and download is logged. *Payoff:* Prevents an angry parent or a legal problem over a child's face going public, and saves editors checking consent forms by hand. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**88. Nightly backup with restore test** — Every night, the app saves a locked (encrypted) copy of the whole database and the photo list to the owner's Google Drive, keeping the last 30 days of copies. Once a month it test-restores a copy to prove the backups work, and it alerts the owner if a night's backup is missing. *Payoff:* A broken server, a stolen phone or an accidental delete can never wipe out years of student, payment and actor records. _(Small effort · Free)_

**89. Unusual activity alarms on Telegram** — The owner and his father get an instant Telegram alert for actions that are rarely innocent: a payment deleted or edited, a big discount, attendance marked for a class that didn't take place, records changed late at night, or one person viewing or downloading many student files at once. *Payoff:* Protects the family business from mistakes and staff dishonesty without the owner having to check everything himself. _(Medium effort · Free)_

**90. Shoot-day check-ins with overtime proof** — On a booked shoot day, the actor taps 'Arrived', 'On set' and 'Wrapped' on their private link, and each tap records the time. Hours beyond the contract are added up so the agency can bill the production, and for children, the guardian can see the status as it happens and gets a Telegram alert. *Payoff:* The agency can bill overtime with proof, and parents trust the school with their children on set. _(Small effort · Free)_

**91. Safe pickup for kids' classes** — Each child has a list of adults approved to collect them, with photos. At the end of class, the coach taps who collected the child, and the office gets an alert if a child is still waiting 15 minutes after class ends. *Payoff:* Protects children and the school's reputation, which is what matters most to Egyptian parents choosing a kids' programme. _(Small effort · Free)_

**92. Staff vetting folder with expiry alerts** — Every coach, editor and chaperone has a checklist: a copy of their national ID, a signed code of conduct and, for anyone working with children, a recent criminal record certificate (فيش وتشبيه). The app sends a reminder before documents expire and can block access to children's sessions until the checklist is complete. *Payoff:* Only checked adults work with children, which protects the students and the family's name. _(Small effort · Free)_

**93. Actors' Syndicate permit tracker** — Each actor's record shows their Actors' Syndicate membership status and expiry date, or whether they need a work permit for each job, plus the approvals needed when a child works. A booking can't be marked confirmed until its permit box is ticked, and expiry dates trigger reminders. *Payoff:* Stops shoots being halted or fined over missing paperwork, a very Egyptian risk that no ready-made tool tracks. _(Small effort · Free)_

**94. One-button staff leaving checklist** — When someone leaves, one button signs them out on every device, removes their access, hands their enquiries, students and tasks to colleagues, and shows any money or cash advances they still hold. Owners can also see which devices are signed in, and can require a PIN to open money screens on shared office tablets. *Payoff:* A departing employee walks away with no access to children's data or the actor list, and none of their work is dropped. _(Small effort · Free)_
