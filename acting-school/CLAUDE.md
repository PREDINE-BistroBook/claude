# Acting School — staff app (school + talent agency)

A standalone project. It shares nothing with the rest of this repository: not the Amico Mio businesses, not `sites/` (Zen etc.). Don't import from, copy from, or "align with" anything outside `acting-school/`. The Notion/Slack/approval rules in the root `CLAUDE.md` are about running those businesses; they don't apply to building this app.

**Whose it is:** Turquoise Studio (Turquoise Casting Studio, Giza; Arabic: استوديو تركواز) — an acting school that sells workshops (ورش) led by named coaches, plus a casting agency. Brand: black + turquoise, the turquoise "T" mark (`BrandMark`; interim drawing until the studio sends the original logo file).

**What it is:** an internal web app (works on phones, installable as a PWA) for the team of an acting school that is also a talent agency. Team accounts only — students and actors don't sign in. English + Arabic, with full right-to-left layout. Its address is `team.turquoisestudio.app` (`staff.turquoisestudio.app`, its first address, forwards there).

**"Team", not "Staff", on screen** (the school's wording, 2026-11-23): every visible text says team / team member(s) — الفريق / عضو الفريق / أعضاء الفريق. "Staff" stays in code names only (`staff.manage`, `/api/staff`, `features/staff/`, this file's "staff app"); `tests/unit/team-web.test.ts` fails if the word shows up in an i18n namespace.

- **School:** students, classes (courses with a weekly schedule → dated sessions), enrollments, attendance.
- **Agency:** talent roster (profiles, headshots, skills, languages), castings with roles, submissions (talent put forward for a role → audition → callback → booked), and **casting opportunities** the team saw on Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp groups or websites and added by hand (`/casting/opportunities`, Part K1: New / Interested / Applied / Not for us, up to 3 screenshots, a red "Asks for money — be careful" flag, "Turn into a casting"; no AI, nothing fetched).
- **Admissions:** enquiries ("leads") from WhatsApp, calls, forms… → follow-ups → interviews (scored on criteria from settings) → decision → enrol (creates the student + enrollment). WhatsApp = `wa.me` links (no API; $0).
- **Everyone:** home dashboard, combined schedule (classes + auditions + admissions interviews).
- **"Add" everywhere** (Part K2): one pattern — a list screen's labelled "+ Add …" is a fixed button at the bottom-end above the tab bar on phones and in the page header on desktop, "Add the first one" in its empty state while the list has nothing at all; a record page's sub-lists each have "+ Add …" at the top of their block; the section homes have quick-action tiles worded like the list buttons ("Add student" on both). Which screen adds what, who sees it and the request it ends in is `layout/addActions.ts`.
- **Three sections** in the staff app — **School** (students, workshops, schedule, admissions), **Casting** (talent, castings, casting applications, audition schedule), **Website** (the public site's content) — switched with tabs at the top; each has its own home, colour and menu (`layout/sections.ts`, `docs/SECTIONS_AND_WEBSITE.md`).
- **Team pack** (in every section): one **Team** page (`/team`: everyone with photo, name and positions; Message; Call to the office with `staff.summon`; with `staff.manage` also Add team member, each member's page and the Active / Inactive filter), one-to-one **Messages**, **Call to the office**, the **Alerts** bell and phone alerts (Web Push), profile photos, photos & files on workshops and castings.
- **Signing in:** with a **username** (the email address works in the same field). A password someone else set — a manager adding a team member or resetting it, the operator's tool — is temporary: the next sign-in shows only "Choose your own password" (Part I of `docs/SECTIONS_AND_WEBSITE.md`).
- **Cafeteria** (in every section, Part J of `docs/SECTIONS_AND_WEBSITE.md`): every team member orders tea, coffee or food from a short menu and says where it should come (`/cafeteria`: "Cafeteria" / «البوفيه» in the account / "More" menu, "Order a drink" on each section home); the **Office helper** (`cafeteria.serve` and no section) has the **orders board** as his home screen (`/cafeteria/board`: On the way → Delivered, or "Can't do this one" with a reason), the **Menu** tab (`/cafeteria/menu`), and is the only one alerted about new orders; the owners have the **monthly consumption page** (`/cafeteria/report`, `cafeteria.report`: who had what, item by item). No prices, no tabs. The `cafeteriaEnabled` setting turns all of it off. **Reception** is a built-in position with no section permissions yet (the school will say what it does).
- **Class videos** (Part L of `docs/SECTIONS_AND_WEBSITE.md`, in School): a coach adds the raw-footage **link** on a session (`videos.add`: "+ Add video" in the session's — or the workshop's — **Videos** block), the **Video editor** works through the queue (`/videos`, `videos.edit`: Start editing → Done with the edited link), the **Video reviewer** (`videos.review`, a built-in position given on top — Moaty) is alerted at once and taps **Approved** or **Needs changes** with a note; the coach is told when it is approved. Videos are links only (the app stores no video). Only the **working** editors and reviewers are alerted (`worksOnVideos`: holding the permission without `staff.manage`) — never the owners or the Executive Director, who holds `videos.edit` + `videos.review` (grant v6, VID-PERM-3) only to keep reach over those accounts. The Videos queue has no "+ Add" (a video is added from its session).
- **Public website** (`/site/...`, or the root of `turquoisestudio.app`): server-rendered, no JavaScript, English + Arabic — workshops with an apply form (→ leads with source `website`), a casting call with photos (→ `casting_applications`, accepted into talent), about & coaches, link-in-bio. **No prices anywhere** (the school's decision): an "Ask about fees on WhatsApp" button instead. **Not public until the owner switches it on** ("Website is public" on the Website home, `siteContent.published`): until then the studio's domain shows a "coming soon" page and only the team's `/site` previews show the site.

## Stack

| Layer | Choice |
|---|---|
| Web | React 19 + Vite 8 + Tailwind 4, React Router 8, TanStack Query 5, i18next |
| API | Hono 4 (`src/server/app.ts`, runtime-agnostic: `createApp({ db, config, files })`). Two targets run the same app: **Node 22** (`src/server/index.ts`) and **Cloudflare** — one Worker named `claude` (`src/worker/index.ts`, `wrangler.jsonc`) serving `dist/web` as static assets and handing `/api/*` to one SQLite Durable Object (`src/worker/school.ts`) |
| Data | SQLite via Drizzle ORM, two synchronous drivers with the same dialect: better-sqlite3 on Node (`$DATA_DIR/app.db`) and drizzle `durable-sqlite` over the Durable Object's storage. Uploaded files behind a `FileStore` (`lib/fileStore.ts`): `$DATA_DIR/uploads/` on Node, the DO's SQLite (`file_chunks`) on Cloudflare, R2 once bound |
| Validation | Zod 4 schemas in `src/shared/schemas/` — the same schema validates the form in the browser and the request on the server |
| Tests | Vitest (API, in-memory DB; every database opened under Vitest enforces the Durable Object limits) in `tests/api/`, unit in `tests/unit/`; Playwright e2e in `tests/e2e/`; `npm run cf:smoke` drives the real Worker in workerd |

```
npm run e2e          # Playwright

```

Cloudflare deploys through Workers Builds (push to the repo) once its build settings are set in the dashboard. Until then, or when it fails, the operator's session deploys from the cloud container with `npm run cf:deploy-embedded` (`wrangler.embedded.jsonc`: the web app bundled into the Worker, because the container's proxy breaks the static-asset upload — see `src/worker/embeddedAssets.ts`); keep `wrangler.embedded.jsonc` identical to `wrangler.jsonc` except `main`/`assets` (the embed script refuses otherwise). Sub-agents and reviewers never run `wrangler deploy`, `wrangler secret`, `wrangler login`, `wrangler dev --remote` or anything else that talks to the Cloudflare account; `cf:dev`/`cf:smoke` strip Cloudflare credentials and run locally (`cf:smoke -- --embedded` checks the bundled variant); both start wrangler with `--infer-origin-from-routes=false` (`wranglerDevArgs` in `scripts/cf-local.ts`) — the configs carry the custom-domain `routes`, and without the flag local wrangler presents every request as the first route's host (`team.turquoisestudio.app`), so the Worker's host routing would never run.

## Rules

### Server
- Every router: `router.use(requireAuth)` or per-route `requirePermission('x.y')`. Permissions are defined in `src/shared/permissions.ts` only (with an EN + AR label and description in `src/web/features/positions/i18n.ts`; a test checks it); a person's effective permissions come from the positions they hold (`SessionUser.permissions`, worked out on every request; owner positions = all). The API is the guard; the UI hiding things is a courtesy. **Giving a built-in position a new permission:** add it to `BUILT_IN_POSITIONS` *and* append a `BUILT_IN_GRANTS` entry with the next version — existing databases get it once at startup (`upgradeBuiltInPositions`), and a permission an owner removes afterwards stays removed; never edit a released entry. **A new built-in position** (Reception, Office helper) is only added to `BUILT_IN_POSITIONS`, at the end, with its own permission list and no grant: `ensureBuiltInPositions` creates it once wherever its `key` is missing (a renamed or changed one is never created again or reset; a position the school already made by hand under the same English or Arabic name is taken over — it gets the key and the built-in's permissions — rather than doubled). A new permission the Executive Director must *not* get is excluded in `EXECUTIVE_DIRECTOR_PERMISSIONS` (as `payments.manage` and `cafeteria.report` are); she got `cafeteria.serve` through `BUILT_IN_GRANTS` v4, `videos.add` + `videos.read` through v5 (with the coaches' `videos.add` and the editor's `videos.edit`; the Video reviewer is a new built-in position, no grant) and `videos.edit` + `videos.review` through v6 (the latest version — VID-PERM-3: she manages the team, so the editor's and the reviewer's accounts stay within her reach; she is never alerted or counted as one, `worksOnVideos`). Which permissions open each section is `SECTION_PERMISSIONS` there too (the web tabs and the server read the same lists).
- Access rules beyond a single permission live in `src/server/lib/access.ts`: without `classes.all` a person only sees their own classes (course teacher, a session's substitute, or the class's creator) and those classes' students plus the students they added — anything else is a 404 (and a student shown from another module, e.g. the student linked to a talent profile, follows the same rule: `maySeeStudent`); attendance needs `courses.read` as well as `attendance.write`; without `students.contact` / `talent.contact` contact fields come back null with `contactHidden: true`, aren't searched, and are ignored on writes. Non-owners never see owner accounts or owner positions — except as a name and photo in the team pack, below — (an owner's id — as a position, teacher or substitute — answers exactly like a missing one) and can't give, take or edit access beyond their own — including by editing a position held by someone who can do more than they can.
- Validate everything: `await body(c, schema)`, `query(c, schema)`, `paramId(c)`. Throw `ApiError` / `fieldError(path, 'ns:errors.key')` / `notFound()` / `forbidden()` / `conflict()` — never return ad-hoc error JSON. Extra data for the client goes in `ApiError`'s `details` (answered as `error.details`, e.g. admissions' `possible_duplicate` → `{ matches }`).
- Admissions: leads are not class-scoped; anyone with `admissions.read` sees them with contact details. Lead status moves on its own (contact on new → contacted, interview scheduled/done/no-show, enrol) and every change writes a `status_change` activity; closed leads (enrolled/declined/lost) have no follow-up. Assignees and interviewers go through `isVisibleActiveStaff` (hidden owners answer like missing ids). Phones are stored normalised (`reqPhoneText`/`optPhoneText`: Western digits, no pasted bidi marks) and matched as international digits (`internationalPhone`); in SQL use the stored `phone_digits` column (students, leads), never a hand-made `replace()` chain — and every insert/update that sets `phone` on those tables goes through `withPhoneDigits(values)`. Write handlers read the body first, then the rows they change inside the transaction (nothing awaits in between).
- Response shapes must match the interfaces in `src/shared/schemas/<module>.ts`. Lists answer `{ items: [...] }`. Create answers 201 with the created resource. Delete answers 204 (`c.body(null, 204)`).
- Multi-step writes go in `db.transaction((tx) => { … })`. **Transactions are synchronous on both drivers (better-sqlite3; `transactionSync` on the Durable Object): no `await` inside** (do async work — hashing, file IO — before).
- Dates are strings: `YYYY-MM-DD`, `HH:MM`, `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`, school-local, no time zone. Use `src/shared/dates.ts`; never `new Date('2026-01-05')`. **On the server, "today" and "now" are `schoolToday(c)` / `schoolNowTime(c)` (`lib/clock.ts`, the school's time zone, `SCHOOL_TIME_ZONE`, default Africa/Cairo) — never `todayISO()`/`nowTime()`/`new Date().getHours()`: Workers run in UTC.** The browser keeps `todayISO()`/`nowTime()` (its own clock).
- Files: through `c.get('files')` (a `FileStore`), never `node:fs` in shared code. Files are private: only `GET /api/files/:id` to signed-in staff — images inline, everything else as an `attachment` download with its name, always `nosniff`. Uploads are typed from their bytes (`lib/fileTypes.ts`: JPEG/PNG/WebP, PDF, .docx/.xlsx/.pptx; never SVG/HTML), ≤ 8 MB. A field that must be a picture checks the stored mime is an image. The one exception to "private" is `GET /site/media/:id`, which serves only what `isPublicFile`/`findPublicFile` (`lib/siteContent.ts`) allow: the site's main photo, visible coaches' photos and covers of workshops that appear on the site — images only. Casting-application photos are never public, and a site image can't be a file that anything outside the website uses — a student's, a talent's or a team member's photo, a workshop's or a casting's file, a chat attachment (`assertSiteImage` → `fileUsedOffSite`, the same `FILE_REFERENCES` list minus the site's own columns): `website.manage` alone never publishes someone else's file by its id.
- **File ids are capabilities.** They're random UUIDs, and `GET /api/files/:id` only checks that you're signed in, so a file id is only ever put in a response for people who may see the record it belongs to (a chat attachment to the conversation's two members, a workshop file to whoever sees the workshop, …). Attaching a file somewhere (`claimUploads` in `lib/fileRefs.ts`) takes only the caller's own recent upload that isn't used anywhere yet — never an id copied from elsewhere, which would show it to more people. Deleting a record that holds a file goes through `releaseFile(s)` after the transaction (or `takeUnusedFiles` inside it, then `deleteStoredFiles` — as deleting a casting application, a talent profile or a talent photo does): the file goes only when nothing in `FILE_REFERENCES` (nor the site's hero photo) still uses it, so **a file goes with its last user** — an accepted applicant's photos are deleted for good whichever of the application or the talent profile is deleted first. That list is the only "is it still used?" check; never write a second one. A new column pointing at `files` goes into `FILE_REFERENCES` (a test compares it with the schema) and gets a case in `referenceCases` in `tests/api/files.test.ts`. An upload that is never attached is taken back by its uploader (`DELETE /api/files/:id`: uploader only, 409 once attached; the web app calls it via `discardUpload` when a picked file is removed or a photo replaced before saving) or swept a day later (`sweepUnusedUploads`, run by `POST /api/files`, ≤ 20 per call); uploads are limited to `UPLOADS_PER_HOUR` per person.
- Contact details stay behind their permission wherever they travel: "Add to roster" puts the guardian's name in the talent's notes, never the guardian's phone or the applicant's raw links (those stay on the application, behind `talent.contact`). An accepted application is closed (409) while its talent profile exists; if that profile is deleted it is open again (`isOnRoster`).
- **Owners in the team pack (a deliberate exception):** the Team page's list (`/api/team`), chat and "call to the office" show owner accounts to everyone as a name and a photo (`PersonRef`), and anyone can message them. Everything about positions and access stays hidden from non-owners (`/api/team` gives an owner no positions; `/api/staff` — the management side of the Team page — still leaves owner accounts out for a non-owner, so their rows have no link to an account page).
- Background work after a response (push notifications): `queuePush(c, …)` from `lib/push` after the transaction — it runs through `c.get('waitUntil')` (the Durable Object's `ctx.waitUntil`; on Node a promise whose errors are logged) and calls out with `c.get('outboundFetch')`. Never await it in the request, never inside a transaction. Text the server writes for a person (notifications, push) is in their account's language, from the EN + AR table in `lib/notifications.ts`. A push subscription belongs to the sign-in session that sent it (`push_subscriptions.session_id`): code that deletes sessions uses `endSession` / `deleteSession` / `signOutUser` (middleware/auth.ts), which delete that session's subscriptions too, and sending only goes to subscriptions whose session still exists and hasn't expired — a signed-out phone or shared computer gets nothing.
- **Push config (Web Push, `lib/push/`):** `config.vapid` comes from `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY` (a var: in `vars` of *both* wrangler configs, since a dashboard-only var is dropped by the next `wrangler deploy`; env on Node) and `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` (a secret; env on Node), base64url P-256 as `npm run vapid-keys` prints them. Both or nothing: one missing or an invalid pair logs one line (never the key) and `config.vapid` is null — push is off (`GET /api/push/config` answers `publicKey: null`, subscribing is a 409, `queuePush` does nothing) while the bell works as usual; tests pass keys with `makeTestContext({ config: { vapid } })`. The private key is never logged, returned or stored in the database. The VAPID subject is `pushSubject(db)`: `mailto:` the website's contact email, else `https://turquoisestudio.app`. The crypto is WebCrypto only (RFC 8291 aes128gcm + RFC 8292 ES256 JWT; `tests/unit/push.test.ts` checks the RFC 8291 example) — no `node:crypto` there. Sending: only to subscriptions whose endpoint passed `isPushServiceEndpoint` (the browsers' push services, https, no port: the server never POSTs to an address a user typed); payload `{ title, body, link, kind }` ≤ 3 KB (`MAX_PUSH_PAYLOAD_BYTES`) with message text cut to a 100-character preview; 404/410 deletes the subscription, other failures count and it goes after `MAX_PUSH_FAILURES` (10).
- **The cafeteria (`/api/cafe`, `lib/cafe.ts`, Part J).** Ordering needs no permission (every signed-in member: `GET /menu`, `POST /orders`, `GET /orders/mine`, `POST /orders/:id/cancel` for their own order while it is `new`); the board and the status buttons need `cafeteria.serve` (`GET /orders?status=`, `POST /orders/:id/status`: the Office helper, the director since grant v4, owners); the menu and the places are edited with `cafeteria.serve` **or** `settings.manage`; the monthly page `GET /report?month=YYYY-MM` needs `cafeteria.report` (owners only out of the box) — delivered orders by the day they were delivered in the school's time zone (`epochAt` month bounds), counted in SQL (`cafeReport`: a few bound parameters however many orders), per person and item plus per-item totals; cancelled orders don't count, people who left are still listed, no prices. An order is seen only by the person who ordered and by whoever serves (someone else's order id is a 404). Status moves one step at a time — `new → on_the_way → delivered` — or to `cancelled` (the orderer while `new`; whoever serves, **with a reason**, while `new` or `on_the_way`); nothing changes after `delivered` / `cancelled`; every move is an `UPDATE … WHERE status = <the status read>` so two taps can't both win. Order lines copy the item's two names (`cafe_order_items.name_en/_ar`): renaming or deleting a menu item never changes an old order. A configured place is stored by its English name (`storedCafePlace`) and shown in the reader's language (`cafePlaceLabel`); item names likewise (`cafeItemName`) — in alerts too. Limits: 10 orders an hour per person, 10 lines an order, 10 of an item a line, 100 menu items, 20 places. Alerts through `notify` + `queuePush` like the team pack: a new order → every **active** account that **serves from the board** (`cafeServers` → `servesFromBoard` in shared/permissions.ts: `cafeteria.serve` and no School/Casting/Website section — the Office helper; **never the owners or the director**, the school's decision, nor the person ordering), in each one's language, cleared from all their bells once the order is taken or cancelled (`markNotificationsReadForEveryone`) and its push replaced quietly (`queueCafeOrderGone`); new-order alerts are hidden from anyone else's bell (`hiddenNotificationKinds`) and while the cafeteria is off; each step → the person who ordered, one notification per order, updated (`replace`). Switching the cafeteria off, or an account off, cancels the open orders concerned (`cancelOpenCafeOrders`, ready-made reasons `closed` / `left`). Ready-made reasons and the sugar/milk chips are stored by their English words and shown in the reader's language (`CAFE_READY_REASONS`, `CAFE_OPTION_CHIPS`, `cafeReasonLabel`, `cafeOptionsLabel`). The `cafeteriaEnabled` setting (default on; `PATCH /api/settings`, `settings.manage`) switches the whole feature off: every `/api/cafe` route answers 409 `cafeteria_off` (one middleware at the top of the router) and nobody is alerted. The poll (`GET /api/chat/unread`) carries `cafe: { enabled, newOrders, myOpenOrders }` (`cafeUnread`) — that is how the web app knows whether to show anything, and the board's badge. **No prices and no tabs**, in the data or on screen. "Today" on the board (`deliveredAt` / `cancelledAt` since the school's midnight) uses `epochAt(config.timeZone, schoolToday(c))`.
- **The video pipeline (`/api/videos`, `lib/videos.ts`, Part L).** Videos are links (`videoLink`: http/https only, ≤ 1000 — never `javascript:`/`data:`; the web shows each link's host and opens it in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`). Four permissions: `videos.read` (see the pipeline: the director, the Video reviewer), `videos.add` (the coaches and the director: add a video to a session — or a workshop — they can see, class scope: courses.read + `courseScope`, anything else a 404), `videos.edit` (the Video editor: take, hand in), `videos.review` (the **Video reviewer** position: approve, ask for changes with a note, reopen an approved one with a note) — owners hold all four, and the director `videos.edit` + `videos.review` too since grant v6 (VID-PERM-3: she manages the team, so she keeps reach over those accounts and may do any step); any of them opens the School section (`SECTION_PERMISSIONS`), where `/videos` is. Who sees what is `videoScope`: read/edit/review (and owners) every video; a coach their workshops' videos and the ones they added. Students in a video follow the student rules (students.read + scope; the rest are only counted, `studentsHidden`). Status `new → editing → in_review → approved`, or `in_review → changes_requested → editing → …`, `approved → changes_requested` (reopen); every move is an `UPDATE … WHERE status = <read>` (a take-over also checks the editor read: an editor who **can no longer edit** — account switched off, *or* the Video editor position taken away while the account stays (`editorsWhoCanEdit`, answered as `Video.editorCanEdit`) — leaves an `editing` video to the others) and a refused move answers 409 `video_moved` with the video as it is now (`details.video`). Adding a video and every step answer `VideoStepResult` — the video plus `told: PersonRef[]`, who was alerted — so the screen names them ("Moaty got an alert") or says nobody was. PATCH / DELETE only while `new` (the coach who added it, still holding videos.add, a reviewer, an owner; 409 `video_locked` after). Every move and comment goes into `video_events` (the history). **Alerts** (`notifyVideo` in the transaction, `pushVideo` after it, in each reader's language, one per video and kind, `replace`) go to the **working** editors and reviewers only — **`worksOnVideos(held, 'videos.edit' | 'videos.review')`** in `shared/permissions.ts`: holds the permission and **not `staff.manage`** (owners hold it — "never owners" —, so does the director), the one rule for the server's alerts and counts and the web; `videoTeam` adds "active": a new video → every working editor; a finished edit → every working reviewer (pushed `high`); "needs changes" / reopen → the editor who did it while a working editor (`videoChangesRecipients`; else — unassigned, switched off, position taken away, or a team manager did the edit — every working editor); approved → the coach who added it (an owner too, when they added it). Nobody is alerted about their own step; comments alert nobody. Never test for "is an owner" in a video alert or count: use `worksOnVideos`. Taking a video marks its `video_new` / `video_changes` alerts read for everyone, a decision its `video_review` alerts; deleting a video — or the workshop that holds it — deletes its alerts (`deleteVideoAlerts`). Video alerts of a part of the work someone no longer does are left out of their bell (`hiddenVideoKinds`, through `hiddenNotificationKinds`): the editor's without `videos.edit`, the reviewer's without `videos.review`, "approved" without any videos permission. The poll carries `videos: VideoCounts | null` (`videoCounts`: the editor's `toEdit` / `dueToday` / `late`, the reviewer's `toCheck` — only for working editors / reviewers, null for owners and the director —, the coach's `addedByMe` / `addedOpen` / `approvedThisWeek` in the school's week, and `reviewers` — how many working reviewers there are (`videoTeam`) — for `videos.edit` holders and whoever holds `staff.manage`: 0 means "Done" alerts nobody, which the screens say). - **Casting opportunities (`/api/opportunities`, Part K1).** `castings.read` lists and opens them (and their screenshots); `castings.write` adds, edits, moves (any status to any status; a move away from `new` records `reviewed_by_id`, back to `new` clears it), deletes and turns one into a casting. Only the title is required; `link` is http/https only. Screenshots: ≤ `OPPORTUNITY_MAX_FILES` (3) images claimed with `claimUploads` (`imagesOnly`) at create; a PATCH `fileIds` is the whole new list — ids already there stay, new ones are claimed, the missing ones are released after the transaction. `POST /:id/to-casting` makes an open casting (title, type mapped by `OPPORTUNITY_CASTING_TYPE`, location, deadline, the notes with roles / link / contact / source in the caller's language — `castingNotesFrom`), copies the screenshots into the casting's files (same audience), links it and moves a `new` one to `interested`; 409 `already_casting` (details `{ castingId }`) while that casting exists (`casting_id` is set null when it is deleted). Deleting an opportunity frees its screenshots unless the casting uses them. The dashboard's `newOpportunities` and `GET /counts` (the Casting menu's badge) count `new` ones.
- Sign-in: failed sign-ins go through `lib/signInBrake.ts` (per account + address, per account, per address, school-wide; address = `c.get('clientIp')`), checked **before** any password hashing — on Cloudflare scrypt blocks the one DO. First-run setup needs the setup code (ADMIN_TOKEN) where `config.requireSetupCode` (always on Cloudflare: the Worker is public from the first deploy); compare secrets with `sameSecret`. The session cookie is `sessionCookieName(config)` (`__Host-as_session` over HTTPS) — never hard-code the name.
- **Sign-in is by username or email address — never hard-code that it is by email.** One field (`loginInput.username`; `email` is its old name, still read for an installed app that hasn't updated): a name with an "@" is looked up as an email address, any other as a username (`findSignInAccount`, `lib/usernames.ts`). The order in `POST /api/auth/login` is fixed: resolve the name (one indexed read) → the brakes under `signInBrakeNames` (the account's email address whichever name was typed, so the brakes stay **per account** — plus its username's automatic address when its email is another one; a name that is nobody's in its email form, a typed address also under the automatic address of its first part — so the *other* name that would lead to an account meets the same tripped brake whether or not the account exists) → one hash (the dummy hash for nobody) → the same 401 for a wrong password, an unknown name and a deactivated account. Nothing before the hash may answer differently for a name that exists (`tests/api/usernames.test.ts` probes it: ten failures under one name, then the other). Code that looks an account up from something a person typed (lockout recovery, operator tools) uses `findSignInAccount`, never `eq(users.email, …)`.
- **Usernames** (`shared/usernames.ts` has the rules: 3–20 of `a–z 0–9 . _ -`, starting with a letter and ending with a letter or digit, never two of `. _ -` in a row — so `<username>@<domain>` always passes `z.email()` —, lower-case, unique; the zod `username` in `schemas/common.ts`). **`RESERVED_USERNAMES`** (admin, administrator, hostmaster, postmaster, webmaster, info, abuse, root, …: addresses the studio's domain itself needs) are nobody's username — refused for managers, setup and the operator too (`errors.usernameReserved`, `status: 'reserved'` in the live check), skipped by the backfill and the suggestions (admin → admin2). **`ROLE_USERNAMES`** (ceo, cfo, owner, director, …) a manager or the operator may give, but nobody takes one for themselves in My profile (400 `errors.usernameForManagers`). `users.username` is nullable in SQL only because SQLite can't add a NOT NULL column to a table with rows: **every insert sets it** and `initDb` fills it for older rows (`backfillUsernames`: the email's first part, numbered on a clash, `user<id>` when nothing usable — deterministic). An account gets or changes its username and email only through `newAccountNames` / `usernameChange` (`lib/usernames.ts`): the email defaults to `<username>@<settings.emailDomain>` (default `turquoisestudio.app`), and an address that was the automatic one follows a new username. A clash answers 409 `username_taken` on `username` and **never says whose it is** — it may be an owner's, whom a non-owner can't see (the same for `GET /api/staff/username-check`, whose `exceptId` counts only for an account the caller may open: an owner's id, for a non-owner, is ignored like a missing one). Usernames go out in `/api/auth/me` and the staff payloads (`staff.manage`), not in `/api/team` or the directory.
- **A password someone else set is temporary** (`users.must_change_password`): set by `POST /api/staff`, by a manager's reset (`PATCH /api/staff/:id` with `password`, not for your own account) and by operator tools; never by setup, and the demo seed clears it. The gate is `requireOwnPassword`, mounted in `app.ts` right after `/api/auth`: such a session gets 403 `password_change_required` from **every route registered below it** — a new router goes below that line, never above — while `/api/auth/*` still answers (`/auth/me` reports `mustChangePassword: true`). `POST /api/auth/choose-password { newPassword }` lifts it: the normal policy (min 8), not the temporary password again, then the flag clears and the person's other sessions end (`signOutUser`). "Not the temporary one again" is always checked against the stored hash (`verifyPassword`), never by comparing strings — scrypt normalises (NFKC), so "ｓalma123" is "salma123" — in `POST /api/auth/password` too. A temporary password itself may be shorter than the policy when an operator tool sets it (sign-in takes any length); a manager's reset keeps the policy. `tests/api/first-password.test.ts` walks the app's route table, so a route in front of the gate fails the suite.

The cafeteria's orders and their lines go (`all`), like every alert in `notifications`, and every class video with who is in it and its history (`videos`, `video_students`, `video_events`: `all`). **A new table goes into that list in the same change** (and into `WIPE_ORDER` in `db/wipe.ts`); `tests/api/clear-demo.test.ts` walks the schema and fails for a table that is missing, out of order, or still holding rows after a run — give the new table a row in that test's demo school too. A new column pointing at `files` goes into `FILE_REFERENCES` as always: the run finds the files to delete through it (`takeEveryUnusedFile`). The tool's own rules: a dry run by default that only reads (`planClearDemo`) and names every account that goes (`willDeleteAccounts`) and what to look at twice (`warnings`: a temporary password several new accounts share — an owner's included —, a new account taking the name of one that goes); a real run needs the dry run's `confirm` code (an HMAC keyed with the ADMIN_TOKEN over the request and the plan, the ids of the accounts that go and each emptied table's last rowid included, so a code works once — never log it or the request, which holds passwords) and plans again inside its one transaction; passwords are hashed and the restore point recorded *before* the transaction; at its end the row counts are checked against the plan and any difference rolls everything back. Restore points come from the runtime through `createApp({ restore })` (`lib/restorePoint.ts`): the Durable Object's point-in-time recovery on Cloudflare (`src/worker/pointInTimeRecovery.ts` — not available in local `wrangler dev`), a copy of the database file on Node (`nodeRestore.ts`). Shared code never calls `ctx.storage` or `node:fs` for it.

### Shared code must run in a Durable Object
`src/server` (except `index.ts`, `nodeConfig.ts`, `nodeFileStore.ts`, `nodeRestore.ts`, `db/client.ts` and the CLI scripts `db/seed.ts`, `db/grantOwner.ts`) runs on the Node server *and* inside the Cloudflare Durable Object, against the same SQL. SQLite there has limits better-sqlite3 doesn't — the first two are enforced on every database opened under Vitest (`durableObjectLimits` in `lib/sqlLimits.ts`, attached by `createDb` when `ACTING_SCHOOL_DO_LIMITS=1`, which `vitest.config.ts` sets; startup steps and the seed subprocess included), so the suite fails where Cloudflare would:
- **At most 100 bound parameters per statement.** Multi-row inserts: `for (const batch of insertBatches(table, rows)) tx.insert(table).values(batch).run()`. IN lists that grow with the data: `inChunks(ids, (part) => …where(inArray(col, part))…)` (or a subquery). Fixed lists (statuses) are fine.
- **LIKE/GLOB patterns of at most 50 bytes** (an Arabic letter is 2): searches use `contains(col, term)` (`instr(lower(col), lower(term)) > 0`), never `like '%term%'`.
- **No custom SQL functions** (nothing like better-sqlite3's `sqlite.function`): compute in JS and store a column (as `phone_digits`, filled on write plus a startup backfill in `initDb`).
- **`.run()` returns nothing on the Durable Object** (the `DB` type makes it `unknown`): to know whether a row was deleted/updated, use `.returning({ id: t.id }).get()`.
- **Errors carry no `.code`**: check constraint failures with `sqliteConstraint(err)` (`lib/errors.ts`; app.ts maps them to 409/400).
- No PRAGMAs except the harmless ones (`createDb` sets WAL/busy_timeout/foreign_keys for the Node file only; foreign keys are on by default in a DO). No `BEGIN`/`SAVEPOINT` SQL: use `db.transaction`.
- No `node:fs`, `process.env` or native modules: Node-only code stays in the files listed above. `node:crypto`, `node:buffer`, `node:zlib` work (nodejs_compat).
- Migrations: `npm run db:generate` writes the SQL *and* `migrations/bundle.ts`; both runtimes apply the bundle at startup (`initDb`, in the DO's `blockConcurrencyWhile`). A test fails if the bundle is out of step. Check generated SQL for table rebuilds (`PRAGMA foreign_keys=OFF` + copy): inside the DO's single migration transaction that pragma does nothing. **Released migrations are frozen** (the live database has applied them and holds real accounts): a change is a new migration that only adds — `ALTER TABLE … ADD COLUMN`, `CREATE INDEX` — and a column that must end up filled is added nullable (or with a default) and filled by a backfill in `initDb`, as `0007_usernames` does (`npx drizzle-kit generate --name <what>` then `npm run db:bundle` names the file). The live database is at `0000`–`0008`. Not yet live: `0009_videos` (three new tables: `videos`, `video_students`, `video_events`) and `0010_opportunities` (two: `casting_opportunities`, `casting_opportunity_files`), both add-only; tests find a migration by its tag (`endsWith('_videos')`, as the cafeteria's by `_cafeteria`) — a migration may be renumbered at a merge, so nothing depends on its number.
- One DO serves every request one at a time (like one Node process); scrypt runs in it (~90 ms per hash, and it doesn't yield: nothing else runs meanwhile) — fine at this size for real sign-ins, which is why anonymous paths must never reach a hash unchecked (sign-in brakes first, setup code first). Request bodies are capped (1 MB JSON, 8 MB uploads): the DO has 128 MB for the whole school.
- Server-side epoch boundaries ("since midnight on …") use `epochAt(config.timeZone, date)`, never `parseDate(date).getTime()` (that's the runtime's midnight: UTC on Workers).
- Don't use `.default()` in zod schemas (PATCH uses `.partial()`); apply defaults in insert code.
- Schema change → edit `db/schema.ts`, then `npm run db:generate`. Never edit generated SQL by hand.

### Public website (`src/server/site/`, mounted at `/site`)
- Same code on Node and in the Durable Object, no session, no `X-Requested-With`. **No client JavaScript**: plain HTML forms, POST → 303 → GET.
- Render with `hono/html` (`html` escapes every interpolation). **Never `raw()` with stored or submitted text** — only fixed markup (icons, the brand mark). Multi-paragraph texts go through `paragraphs()`.
- Every visible string in `site/i18n.ts`, both languages; `<html lang dir>` from `:lang`. Number fields accept Arabic-Indic digits (`blankToNullNumber`); the date of birth is three plain lists (`dobField`: day, month, year — never `type="date"`), joined in `submit.ts`.
- **Paths:** build every link with `pathsFor(c)` / `sitePathsFor(url, config.publicSiteHosts)` from `site/paths.ts` — never hard-code `/site`. On a public host (`PUBLIC_SITE_HOSTS`: turquoisestudio.app, www → 301 apex) the site is at the root and the Worker/Node map `/x` to the app's `/site/x`; everywhere else it is under `/site` (previews). Nothing of the staff app answers on a public host; the staff app's own host is `team.turquoisestudio.app` (`staff.` forwards to it: `HOST_REDIRECTS`, `src/worker/hostRedirect.ts`, before any other routing). With `PUBLIC_SITE_HOSTS` set, the links the staff app shows and copies (`GET /api/site/summary`, `siteUrls`) are `https://<first host>/<lang>/…`, and a `/site` preview's canonical / hreflang / og:url name that domain too; without it, the request's own origin + `/site/…` (local runs, tests).
- **Forms** go through `guard()` in `site/submit.ts`, in this order: Origin must be our host (else 403), body cap (64 KB enquiries, 20 MB casting form; the Worker's `maxBodyBytes` refuses a larger Content-Length first — a new `/site` POST route is capped at 64 KB unless added there), **rate limits before a byte of the body is read — with or without a Content-Length** (`siteFormRateLimited`), then a capped read with a deadline, a bounded number of multipart parts and bounded text fields (nothing over 4,000 characters reaches validation or is echoed), honeypot field `website` (fake success, nothing stored), signed form token (`formToken`: ≥ 3 s, ≤ 24 h, HMAC with the `siteFormSecret` setting, constant-time). Then validate — zod (`shared/schemas/site.ts` / `castingApplications.ts`), the guardian rules and the photos **in one round** (every form that comes back costs the applicant their photos) — and save through `createWebsiteLead` / `createCastingApplication` (photos: real JPEG/PNG/WebP by magic bytes, ≤ 6 MB each, 1–3; stored photos are deleted again if a later step fails).
- **The limits count two different things** (`lib/siteForms.ts`). *Attempts*: every POST of one client address, counted before reading — enquiries 20/hour, casting 12/hour — so honest mistakes don't lock anyone out but junk is bounded. *What is stored*: per address (enquiries 5/hour, casting 3/hour), school-wide (300/day, 100/day) and casting photo bytes kept (300 MB/day) — looked at before reading (full → 429), **counted only at the write** (`countSiteFormSubmission`, no `await` between the count and the write), with the photos' real sizes. So a request that stores nothing (no token, a validation error, a declared length with no body) never uses up the school's day. Per-address keys are the IPv4 address or the IPv6 **/64** (`siteAddressKey`). Casting bodies in flight are capped (`CASTING_IN_FLIGHT_MAX_BYTES`, 40 MB together; beyond it the "busy" 429 page, nothing read or counted): it is the one anonymous route that holds megabytes, and the DO has 128 MB. A flood from many addresses is for a Cloudflare rate-limiting rule (operator setting), not code.
- **Redirects never leave the site:** build a `Location` from the URL's still-encoded path (`new URL(c.req.url).pathname`), never from `c.req.path` (Hono decodes it: `%5C`, `%09`, `%0D%0A`). A form's thank-you redirect ends in `#thanks`; an error page's "Back to the form" link goes through `backToForm()` (`paths.ts`: `?again=1#apply`, a real navigation even when the form posts to its own address).
- **The same phone twice** (`createWebsiteLead`): only the *same person* (`sameLeadPerson`: first name, then date of birth or family name) is folded into the open lead — as a note with the date of birth and email, and the follow-up made due today. Anyone else on that phone (a parent's second child) gets a lead of their own, noting the other enquiry.
- Headers on every `/site` response (`site/headers.ts`): the CSP (`default-src 'none'`…), nosniff, Referrer-Policy; pages `public, max-age=60`, form responses, error and thank-you pages `no-store`.
- **The publish switch** (`siteContent.published`, "Website is public" on the Website home; default false, in the `site` settings JSON — no column): while it is off, a public host answers only the "coming soon" page (`comingSoonPage`: the studio's name and the saved WhatsApp lines, Instagram and address — never other content) — 200 at `/` with noindex and `no-store`, every other GET → 302 to `/` (a fixed Location), `/media/*` 404, every POST the same page with 503 before its body is read (nothing stored or counted). That is one middleware at the top of `site/index.ts`; pages and forms never check the switch themselves. `/site/…` on any other host is the preview and always renders the whole site, forms working, with the banner from `layout({ preview })` (`ctx.preview`), noindex and `no-store` while it is off. `PUT /api/site/content` without `published` keeps the stored value (texts never publish or unpublish) — and the Website screens rely on that: every save goes through `withContentChange` (`features/website/utils.ts`), which leaves `published` out unless the switch itself is being turned, so a save that arrives late can't undo a switch someone else just turned; `saveSiteContent` without it stores false, which is what the demo seed wants. A test that needs the public site as visitors see it saves `published: true` first.
- The public site shows only published content (`loadPublicWorkshops`: visible + active + not over; visible coaches); files only through `/site/media/:id` (see Files above).
