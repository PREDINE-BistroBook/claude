Reviewing the preview?

Welcome. Four things to do first.
  1. Add your team in Settings: one account per person, locked to their city.
  2. Check prices and session names with us; they're placeholders until confirmed.
  3. Add today's bookings by hand (Bookings → Add a booking) so the statistics and the clients' loyalty count start from day one.
  4. Share the site: put zenrecovery.club in the Instagram bio. Clients who register get their invite link and rewards automatically.
How to read this page
  • Sessions counts bookings that are reserved, confirmed or done. Cancelled and missed ones are not counted.
  • Revenue is what clients actually paid, after rewards. Euro and Egyptian pound are always shown separately.
  • Clients are people with at least one session; "new" means their first session was this month.
  • Upcoming is everything reserved or confirmed from today onwards. Hover any bar for the exact number; "Show as table" lists them.

Bookings

How bookings work
  1. A client books online: it appears as Reserved. Message them on WhatsApp, agree the hour, then click Confirm and type the hour in the booking (click the row).
  2. After the session, click Done. This is what counts the session for their free-session reward, so don't skip it. Then open the row and write a note to the client: what you did, what to do at home. They see it in their account, and they can rate the session back.
  3. Didn't show up? Missed. Cancelled in time? Cancel (their reward, if they used one, is given back).
  4. Walk-ins, WhatsApp bookings and cash: Add a booking. If the email matches an account, it counts for their rewards too.
  5. A red ! next to a name means the client told us about a health condition. Open the row and read it before the session.

Calendar

How live time slots work
  • Until you add opening hours for a city, clients pick a rough window (morning / afternoon / evening) and you agree the hour on WhatsApp. That's fine to start with.
  • Add opening hours below (for example Mon–Sat 10:00–18:00, one-hour slots) and the website shows exact free times instead. A time disappears the moment someone books it, so no double bookings.
  • Hours can be for the whole room or for one therapist. If you add therapists in the Team tab, clients can choose who they see.
  • Holiday, a dentist appointment, an afternoon off: add it under Days off. A whole day, or just a few hours.
  • Waiting list: clients who wanted a full day get an email by themselves if a time opens up there.

Team

Why add therapists
  • Clients see the team on the booking page, can pick a favourite, and their preferred therapist is preselected next time.
  • Each booking can be assigned to a therapist (open a booking). Opening hours and days off can be per person.
  • This is the public profile only. Sign-in accounts for the team are under Settings → Who can sign in.

Packs, partners and gifts

About packs
  • A pack is several sessions paid up front at a lower price per session, valid for a number of months. Clients buy them in their account; when they book they pick "Use my pack" and nothing is charged.
  • The platform fee is taken once, on the pack purchase. Sessions used from a pack show as "from pack" in Bookings.
  • Owner only: create, pause or remove packs. Everyone can see what's been sold.

  • Give a hotel, hostel, gym or dive centre a code. Their guests type it when booking and get the discount; you see exactly how many bookings each partner brings, so you know who deserves a bigger thank-you.
  • A code can be for one city or all of them. Pause it any time.

Needs a therapist's OK

What this is
  • When a client tells us they're pregnant, take blood thinners, have a bleeding or heart condition, or had recent surgery, the website doesn't take their money. The booking lands here instead.
  • Read their answers, message them on WhatsApp if you need to, then Approve (the booking becomes Confirmed, they pay at the session, and future bookings go straight through) or Cancel.
  • Clients who filled in the questionnaire with a red flag but haven't booked yet are listed below too; approving them in advance lets them book online normally.
