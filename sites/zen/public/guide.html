Good to know

Small things that make a session better.

People come to us for muscle tension, recovery after training, and the simple relief of an hour where nothing is asked of them. Cupping sits comfortably alongside physiotherapy and massage. It doesn't replace a doctor.

Talk to your doctor first if you're pregnant, take blood thinners, have a skin condition where the cups would go, or have a heart or blood-clotting condition. If you're unsure, message us and we'll tell you honestly whether cupping is a good idea for you.
Before
Eat something light a couple of hours before. Drink water. Skip a heavy workout that day.
Wear
Anything easy to move out of the way. For a back session you'll be face down with a towel; for legs, shorts are enough.
Feels like
A firm pull, then warmth. It shouldn't hurt. If it does, say so and the cup comes off.
After
Keep warm and drink more water than usual. No cold showers, saunas or swimming for about six hours. Some people feel tired that evening; sleep well.
Marks
Three to ten days. Fine to show, easy to cover. Photos in daylight are entirely optional.
How often
Once every one to two weeks while something is bothering you, then whenever your body asks.

Booking rules

Clear rules, no surprises.

The same rules for every city and every therapist, close to what you know from tour and activity platforms.

  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the session, from your account or on WhatsApp. Moving it is free in the same window.
  • Late cancellation, inside that window: 100% of the price is kept. A reward, gift or package session used for it is not returned.
  • No-show: 100% of the price is kept.
  • The therapist cancels: full refund or a new time, your choice.
  • Late arrival shortens the session; the price stays the same.
  • Health check first: a session sent for a therapist's OK isn't charged until approved and can be cancelled free before that.
  • Refunds go back to the card used, within 5–10 days.
