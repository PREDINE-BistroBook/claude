Booking rules free cancellation up to 24 h before
  • Cancel or move your session free of charge up to 24 hours before it starts, from your account or on WhatsApp.
  • Inside that window, 100% of the price is kept, and a reward, gift or package session used for it is not returned.
  • Not turning up counts as a no-show: 100% of the price is kept.
  • If the therapist has to cancel, you get a full refund or a new time, your choice.
  • Arriving late shortens the session; the price stays the same.
  • Sessions sent for a therapist's OK are not charged until approved and can be cancelled free at any time before.
  • Refunds go back to the card used, within 5–10 days.
